Daily （日常）

Daily is a personal iOS app for everyday life, built for one person's own use. It brings together four small tools in one place:

  • Expense ledger — record daily spending.
  • Subscriptions & bills — keep track of subscriptions and their bills.
  • Charging decisions — decide when and where to charge an EV.
  • Daily planning — weigh the trade-offs of the day's plans, such as travel time for an evening tennis session.

How the Gmail connection works

The app can optionally connect to your own Gmail account, using the Gmail API with the gmail.readonly scope only, to find subscription billing emails. Your emails are read on your device and used only to identify subscription bills. They are not uploaded to, stored on, or processed by any server we operate, and they are never shared or sold.

Connecting Gmail is optional. The app does not require an account with us, shows no ads, and collects no analytics.
