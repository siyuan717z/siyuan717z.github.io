Privacy Policy for Daily （日常）

Effective date: November 13, 2026. Last updated: November 13, 2026.

Daily is a personal iOS app built for one person's own use. This policy describes what the app accesses and what it does with that information.

Information the app accesses

If you choose to connect Gmail, the app reads your Gmail messages through the Google Gmail API using the gmail.readonly scope only. It uses that read-only access solely to identify subscription and billing emails on your device, so the app can list your subscriptions and their bills.

The app does not ask for any other Gmail scope, and it does not read, send, modify, or delete your emails.

How your information is handled

  • Processed on your device. Email content is read and processed on your iPhone. It is not uploaded to, stored on, or processed by any server operated by us.
  • Not shared or sold. We do not share, sell, or disclose your email content to any third party.
  • No advertising or analytics. The app shows no ads and collects no analytics data.
  • No account with us. The app does not require you to create an account, and we do not keep a copy of your data.

Your control

Connecting Gmail is optional, and you can revoke the app's access at any time in your Google Account settings, under third-party access. Revoking access stops the app from reading your Gmail.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new last-updated date.
